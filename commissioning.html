Custom Commissions

Let's Create Something Unique Together

Your Vision, My Artistry

Transform your ideas into stunning custom artwork that perfectly complements your space and reflects your personal style.

Whether you're looking for a statement piece for your home, a meaningful gift, or corporate art that embodies your brand, I work closely with you to bring your vision to life. Each commission is a collaborative journey, ensuring the final piece exceeds your expectations.

The Commission Process

1

Initial Consultation

We discuss your vision, preferences, color scheme, size requirements, and the space where the artwork will be displayed. Share reference images or describe the mood you want to create.

2

Proposal & Quote

Based on our discussion, I provide a detailed proposal including concept sketches, estimated timeline, and pricing. Once approved, a 50% deposit secures your commission slot.

3

Creation & Updates

I begin creating your artwork, providing progress photos at key stages. You can request minor adjustments to ensure the piece aligns with your vision.

4

Completion & Delivery

Upon completion, I share final photos for your approval. After final payment, your artwork is carefully packaged and delivered or made available for pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a commission take?

Timelines vary based on size and complexity, typically ranging from 2-10 weeks. Rush commissions may be available for an additional fee.

What mediums do you work with?

I specialize in acrylic and oil paintings, mixed media, and can incorporate various techniques based on your preferences.

Can I see the artwork during creation?

Absolutely! I provide progress photos at key stages and welcome your feedback to ensure you love the final piece.

What if I'm not satisfied with the final piece?

Your satisfaction is my priority. I work with you throughout the process to ensure the artwork meets your expectations. Minor revisions are included in the commission fee.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! I carefully package and ship artwork worldwide. Shipping costs are calculated based on destination and artwork size.
